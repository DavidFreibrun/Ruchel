# Episode 11: titles and thumbnail

## Why these titles

The channel's two best videos use one frame: a powerful institution erased something Filipino, and the evidence shows what really happened.

- [Spain Banned This Filipino Alphabet — Here's What They Were Afraid Of](https://youtube.com/watch?v=QrcB-ahL4G4): about 47K views
- [Spain Called These Filipino Gods Demons — Here's What Their Own Records Say](https://youtube.com/watch?v=yu8IxnrG0Ok): about 22K views

The diaspora episodes without that frame did much worse: [Berkeley](https://youtube.com/watch?v=evorG2hYC_Y) got about 860 views. Ep 11 is a diaspora story, so the title has to carry the erasure angle.

## Title options (best first)

1. **They Bulldozed America's Biggest Filipino Town — Here's What They Built Instead**
2. America Paved Over Little Manila — The Filipino History Stockton Buried
3. A Freeway Erased America's Little Manila — Here's What the Records Show
4. Why You've Never Heard of Little Manila — America's Biggest Filipino Town
5. The Filipino Town America Called a Slum — The Untold History of Stockton

Pinned-comment question: "What was there before the freeway in your city?"

## Thumbnail concept

- Split frame. On the left is a sepia 1930s street scene: young Filipino men in three-piece suits under a hotel and pool-hall sign. On the right is the same angle today: a grey concrete freeway overpass.
- A torn-paper seam runs down the middle, with a bulldozer blade cutting in from the right.
- Text, 3 words max: **PAVED OVER** (or **ERASED**), in the channel's red.
- Don't show the McDonald's logo or any other brand. A generic gas-station canopy under the overpass makes the point without the trademark risk.

## Description opener (first 2 lines)

For 40 years, a few blocks of Stockton, California were the capital of Filipino America. Then the city called it blight, and the state built a freeway through it.
